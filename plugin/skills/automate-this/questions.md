# What a tool may need to know

Ask only what you cannot see from the example, the role card, `assistant/knowledge.md`, or the sources list. At most three questions per tool, one at a time, in plain words. Each line says what the answer feeds.

## The input

- "What do you start from each time, and where does a fresh copy come from?" Feeds: `data_path`, `manual_steps`, the sources list.
- "Which of these is the official source when they disagree?" Feeds: the facts step and the notes (true of the job: save it).
- "Is anything in it often missing or wrong?" Feeds: input checks and the facts step.

## The result

- "Who reads this, and what do they need from it?" Feeds: the result's shape and the checklist. Who reads a kind of report is true of the job: save it.
- "What would make this result embarrassing or wrong?" Feeds: the checklist.
- "Are there names or words that must be written a certain way?" Feeds: the checklist and the notes (save it).

## The rhythm

- "How often do you do this, and when is it due?" Feeds: `rhythm` and upkeep reminders. A deadline is true of the job: save it.
- "How long does it take you by hand?" Feeds: `your_minutes_by_hand` and the time saved on the tools page.

## The two checks (always settled, outside the three)

- Confidential: see step 3 of the automate-this page.
- Numbers for leaders: see step 3 of the automate-this page.
