# Recipe: Meeting to actions

## When it fits

The person turns meeting notes or a transcript into decisions, action items, and entries for a decision log.

## What to ask

- Ask for one past decision log entry or action list they liked, and copy its format exactly.
- Who should receive the summary?
- Do actions need due dates even when none were said?

## Inputs and checks

- The notes or transcript: `md`, `txt`, or pasted text saved to the inbox.

## Facts step

Usually none.

## The result

- Decisions: what was decided, by whom, in their decision log format.
- Actions: each with an owner and a date. When the notes give no owner or date, write "Owner: unknown" or "Date: unknown"; never invent one.
- Open questions that need an answer, with who raised them.
- A short summary draft for the attendees. Never send it.
- End with "Sources:" naming the notes and the meeting date.

## Checklist items to include

- Every action has an owner and a date, or says unknown.
- Decisions are separate from discussion.
- Decision log entries follow the person's own format.

## Numbers

Repeat numbers exactly as said in the meeting, with who said them.

## Watch out for

- Something discussed is not something decided. Only list a decision when the notes show agreement.
- Names: use the names in the notes; do not guess full names.

## Example requests

- "Turn these notes into actions and decisions."
- "Write up the decisions from today's meeting for the log."
