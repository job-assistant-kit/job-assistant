# Recipe: Tailor for an audience

## When it fits

The person adapts one deck or document for a specific reader: a business unit, a leader, a partner team.

## What to ask

- Who is the audience, and what do they care about most?
- How long should it be?
- Is anything off limits for this audience (confidential plans, partner names, pricing)?

## Inputs and checks

- The original document: `md`, `txt`, or `pdf`, or read through a connected service.

## Facts step

Usually none. Facts are carried over from the original unchanged.

## The result

- The adapted document, with the same facts and numbers as the original.
- Framing, depth, order, and examples changed to suit the audience.
- A short list of what was changed and why, so the person can check it.
- End with "Sources:" naming the original document.

## Checklist items to include

- Every fact and number matches the original exactly.
- Lists what was changed for this audience.
- Leaves out anything the person said is off limits for this audience.

## Numbers

Numbers are copied, never recomputed or rounded. If a number needs updating, say so and ask.

## Watch out for

- Confidential content for an outside audience: check with the person before including it.
- Shortening must not change meaning.

## Example requests

- "Tailor this deck for the services team."
- "Make a one-page version of this for the VP."
