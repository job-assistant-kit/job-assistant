# Role card template

Fill each line in plain words from the job description and the interview. Keep the whole card under 250 words. Write "unknown" for anything you were not told. Do not include confidential details such as unannounced offerings, partner names, or pricing.

```
<Name> is a <job title> at <organization>, working on <area>.

Main work:
- <recurring task> (<how often>, for <who reads it>)
- <recurring task> (<how often>, for <who reads it>)
- <recurring task> (<how often>, for <who reads it>)

Readers and what they need:
- <audience>: <what they need and in what shape>

Main sources of truth:
- <document or data>: <what it is the source for>

Must never go wrong:
- <mistake to avoid>

Confidential topics to keep out of searches and drafts:
- <topic, described generally>

Words and names to use exactly:
- <term>: <how to write it>

Busiest times: <dates or periods>
```
